INSCAPE develops tools to explore, quantify and map the construction of new knowledge across scientific disciplines. Scientific uncertainty, as expressed in publications, is analysed as a discursive trace of exploration: the project identifies the concepts and objects most strongly associated with uncertainty in each discipline, in order to map research fronts, areas of structured speculation and open questions.

The project builds on the linguistic methods developed at CRIT for the annotation of scientific uncertainty (ANR InSciM project, UnScientify system), and articulates Natural Language Processing, philosophy of science and science studies.

**Duration:** November 2026 - October 2028

**Funding:** Région Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, ATRACT programme (hosting of young researchers).

**Team:** Iana Atanassova (PI), Dr Francis Lareau (post-doctoral researcher).
