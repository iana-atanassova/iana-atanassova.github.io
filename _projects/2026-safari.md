Scientific figures often remain inaccessible "black boxes" for blind and low-vision (BLV) readers when they lack meaningful textual alternatives. SAFARI develops a computational framework to audit whether a figure is functionally accessible, by:

- comparing the alt-text, the figure caption and the in-text mentions of the figure (semantic alignment);
- measuring whether key scientific information (quantitative values, statistical indicators, relations) is present in the accessibility metadata (information density);
- generating automated feedback for authors to improve alt-text descriptions.

The project includes a research mobility at the University of La Laguna (COSLING group, Spain).

**Duration:** September 2026 - August 2027

**Funding:** European University Alliance STARS-EU.
