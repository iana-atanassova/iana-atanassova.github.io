Creation of a short lifelong-learning training course "Data Excellence Science" (DES) at Université Marie et Louis Pasteur, to be converted into a university diploma (DU). The course introduces value-driven data governance, combining computer science, management, law and linguistics, with practical work based on the Data Excellence Framework (DEF) and the DEMS platform.

**Duration:** September 2026 - December 2027

**Funding:** RITM-BFC programme (ANR-17-NCUN-0003), call for lifelong learning projects (AMI FTLV).

**Partner:** Global Data Excellence (Switzerland).
