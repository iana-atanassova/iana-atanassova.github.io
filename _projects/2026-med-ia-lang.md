**MED-IA-LANG** — *MÉDiations numériques, Intelligence Artificielle et traitement des LANGues pour l'insertion professionnelle et la communication internationale des entreprises en contexte régional* — studies how to improve language learning and action in intercultural contexts from authentic situations, while taking advantage of artificial intelligence.

The project designs personalised, modular and adaptive learning paths based on authentic content from digital media and professional communication (films, videos, platform excerpts, company documents). It addresses three publics: international residents in integration or training programmes, French speakers learning English or other languages for academic or professional purposes, and company employees, in particular in import-export and international communication.

The approach follows an "AI + human expertise" logic: generative AI, rule-based linguistic methods and case-based reasoning are combined to suggest resources, generate exercises and personalise learning paths, while human validation guarantees linguistic, pedagogical and ethical quality. The project also addresses explainability, traceability and computational sobriety.

**Duration:** October 2026 - September 2029

**Funding:** Région Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, research projects support programme.
