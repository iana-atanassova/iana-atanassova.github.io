The **TEDonnées** workshops ("Techniques émergentes de données", Besançon, France) aim to promote interactions between students, researchers and companies in the field of Natural Language Processing, and to inform the general public. I organised several editions:

- 2024 - Exchanging and innovating: SSH at the horizon of digital technology
- 2022 - Fake news and credibility of public information
- 2018 - Smart cities and language technologies
- 2017 - Data mining and e-reputation
