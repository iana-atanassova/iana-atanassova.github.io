The initiative **"IUF dans le cartable"** (*IUF in the Backpack*) offers high school students the opportunity to meet and talk informally with internationally recognised researchers from across France, all members of the Institut Universitaire de France (IUF). By creating a space for dialogue outside traditional academic settings, it aims to strengthen students' interest in science and research.

In March 2026, I organised the first edition of the event in Besançon. Students from all 28 general high schools of the Besançon academic region took part in these exchanges.

I had previously taken part as a researcher in AI in the 2022 (Nancy) and 2024 (Limoges) editions.

More about the initiative: <a href="https://www.iufrance.fr/liuf-dans-le-cartable.html" target="_blank">iufrance.fr</a>
