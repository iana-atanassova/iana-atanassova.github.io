**« Explorer le multilinguisme dans les références scientifiques avec ISTEX : méthodes et perspectives pour la fouille textuelle »**

Talk (in French) at the national training *ANF Corpus ISTEX - L'infrastructure ISTEX au service de la constitution et de l'analyse de corpus textuels*, organised by INIST in Nancy, November 2025.

I shared my experience in using ISTEX data to analyse bibliographies. The results show the predominance of English as a lingua franca, but also the richness of scientific communication: authors draw on knowledge from articles published in more than fifty other languages.
