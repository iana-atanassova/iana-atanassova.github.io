UnScientify is a linguistic knowledge-based tool for the automatic annotation of scientific articles and for the detection and categorisation of scientific uncertainty. It relies on spaCy and on a set of lexical and morphosyntactic patterns, and was developed as part of the ANR InSciM project (ANR-21-CE38-0003-01).
